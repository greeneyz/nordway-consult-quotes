# Nordway Solutions Hub

I have to make a website for my company registered in finland named nordway consult. What to show on website?
Well it should be modern and professional and highly attractive. Its about providing consulting services in software development and data engineering.
Whats will be different on our website, there are thousands of such websites out there. Well we will have selection of service and select the number of people required and we will give hourly, daily and monthly price in which we can give consultant to them or if consultant not needed then the same prices for just the work.
In frontend any javascript framework. In backend python, nodejs, typescript, .net and java.
Any CI/CD or cloud.
In data engineering data bricks, fabrics, airflow.
Put whatever else you think is more good.
The rates which we will show on the fly should also say plus VAT.
Contact information should not have any contact us form rather just the email address which is info@nordwayconsult.fi
Show me how creative you are!
One more thing, the favicon should not be lovable icon rather whatever logo you make for my company.
